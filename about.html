About

Cyberseum was established in 2026 by an art lover with a passion for it. The site began as a simple idea: create a fun, easy‑to‑browse artworks website where anyone can discover unique work pieces and enjoy them. Every artwork added to Cyberseum is chosen with care, helping people find something new, exciting, or unexpected. The passion project continues to grow, with more artworks being added all the time, all driven by the goal of art accessible, enjoyable, and fun for everyone.
