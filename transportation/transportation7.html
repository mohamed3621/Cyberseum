Steam Engines & Locomotives

Era: 1800s

Steam-powered locomotives, first practically demonstrated by Richard Trevithick in 1804 and commercialized by George Stephenson in the 1820s, collapsed travel times that had remained essentially unchanged for millennia in a matter of decades. Railways didn't just move people faster, they restructured entire economies, allowing raw materials and manufactured goods to move across countries in hours rather than weeks, and directly fueling the broader Industrial Revolution they were born alongside.

Type: Industrial Transportation
