Walking & Early Human Migration

Era: Prehistoric

Long before any form of transportation technology existed, humans relied entirely on walking to migrate out of Africa and across the globe, a process that unfolded over tens of thousands of years and eventually reached every continent except Antarctica. Early humans could reportedly walk enormous daily distances, and this slow, on-foot spread allowed humans to adapt to environments ranging from Arctic tundra to tropical rainforest, developing distinct cultures and technologies along the way as they gradually spread across the planet.

Type: Early Human Mobility
