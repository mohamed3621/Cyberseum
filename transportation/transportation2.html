Animal Domestication & Horseback Travel

Era: 4000 BCE – onward

Horses were likely first domesticated around 3500 BCE on the Eurasian steppes, and the shift from walking to horseback travel was transformative, dramatically increasing how far and fast people, goods, and information could move. Domesticated horses also reshaped warfare entirely, giving rise to mounted cavalry that would dominate battlefields for thousands of years afterward, and made possible vast trade and migration networks, like the later Silk Road, that would have been far slower and riskier on foot.

Type: Early Animal-Based Transport
