The Invention of the Wheel

Era: c. 3500 BCE

The wheel wasn't invented until roughly 3500 BCE, notably later than many other major human innovations like agriculture, writing, and even bronze metallurgy, likely because a functional wheel and axle require a level of precision engineering that's genuinely difficult to achieve. Early wheels were solid wood, heavy, and used mainly for pottery before being adapted for transportation, and the innovation would go on to be considered so fundamental it's still used as a metaphor for foundational technological progress today.

Type: Foundational Mechanical Technology
