Electric Vehicles & Future Transport

Era: 2010s – Future

Electric vehicles aren't actually a recent invention. Early EVs existed alongside gasoline cars in the late 1800s and were briefly quite popular before cheap gasoline and Ford's assembly line made combustion engines dominant for over a century. Modern battery technology has revived the concept entirely, and companies like Tesla have helped push EVs from a niche curiosity back into the mainstream, while research into autonomous driving and urban air mobility hints at further major shifts in how people and goods move in the decades ahead.

Type: Emerging Transportation Technology
