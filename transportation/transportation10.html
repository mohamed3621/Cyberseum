Commercial Air Travel

Era: 1950s – Present

Commercial air travel expanded dramatically after World War II, as surplus aircraft and trained pilots, combined with new jet engine technology introduced in the 1950s, made flying faster, safer, and gradually more affordable. The introduction of wide-body jets like the Boeing 747 in 1970 further slashed the relative cost per passenger, transforming international travel from an exclusive luxury into something accessible to a rapidly growing global middle class within just a couple of decades.

Type: Modern Aviation
