Automobiles & Mass Production

Era: 1900s

Karl Benz built the first true automobile in 1885, but cars remained expensive, hand-built luxuries until Henry Ford's moving assembly line in 1913 slashed production costs and time dramatically, making car ownership realistic for ordinary working families for the first time. This single innovation reshaped cities, suburbs, and daily life across the 20th century, and the assembly line concept itself went on to influence manufacturing far beyond the automotive industry entirely.

Type: Automotive Technology
