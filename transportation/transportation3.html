Ancient Boats & Rafts

Era: 10,000 BCE – onward

The earliest known boats date back over 8,000 years, simple dugout canoes and reed rafts that nonetheless allowed early humans to fish, trade, and migrate along coastlines and rivers in ways walking never could. Remarkably, evidence suggests early humans may have made intentional sea crossings to reach places like Australia over 65,000 years ago, using rafts, a feat that required real planning and knowledge of currents, since land was not visible from the departure point.

Type: Early Water Transportation
