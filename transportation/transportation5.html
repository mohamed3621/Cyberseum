Chariots & Early Carts

Era: 2000 BCE – onward

Chariots emerged around 2000 BCE and quickly became a decisive military technology across Egypt, Mesopotamia, and later Greece and Rome, giving armies dramatically improved speed and mobility on the battlefield. Beyond warfare, simpler carts pulled by oxen or donkeys became essential for agriculture and trade, allowing communities to move goods and harvests far more efficiently than human portage alone, quietly reshaping how ancient economies and cities could grow and organize themselves.

Type: Early Wheeled Transport
