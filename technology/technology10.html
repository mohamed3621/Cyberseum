Mobile Technology & Smartphones

Era: 2000s – Present

The first handheld mobile phone call was made in 1973, but the device weighed over a kilogram and offered just 30 minutes of talk time before needing a 10-hour recharge. The real transformation came with the smartphone era beginning around 2007, when devices combined phone, internet, camera, and computer into a single pocket-sized tool, fundamentally changing how billions of people communicate, work, navigate, and access information, arguably faster and more broadly than any previous technology in history.

Type: Mobile & Wireless Technology
