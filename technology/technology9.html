The Internet Era

Era: 1990s – Present

The internet began as ARPANET, a US military and academic research project in 1969 designed to let computers at different universities communicate and share resources. It remained a niche tool for researchers for over two decades until the invention of the World Wide Web in 1989 and the arrival of graphical web browsers in the early 1990s made it accessible to ordinary people for the first time, triggering one of the fastest and most disruptive technological shifts in human history.

Type: Information Technology
