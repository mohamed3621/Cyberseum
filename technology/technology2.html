The Bronze & Iron Ages

Era: 3300 – 500 BCE

The shift from stone to bronze tools around 3300 BCE required a level of coordination stone-age societies rarely needed, since bronze requires mining, smelting, and combining two separate metals, copper and tin, that often weren't found in the same regions, forcing early trade networks to expand dramatically. The later shift to iron around 1200 BCE was arguably even more significant, since iron ore was far more widely available than tin, making strong tools and weapons accessible to far more people and reshaping warfare and agriculture across multiple continents.

Type: Ancient Metallurgy
