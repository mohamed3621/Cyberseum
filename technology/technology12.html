Future Tech & Innovation

Era: Near Future

Emerging technologies like quantum computing, which uses quantum mechanical properties to potentially solve certain problems exponentially faster than classical computers, and advances in biotechnology, like CRISPR gene editing, are poised to reshape fields from medicine to cryptography in the coming decades. Meanwhile, renewable energy technology and reusable spacecraft have made ambitions like sustainable power grids and interplanetary exploration genuinely realistic for the first time, rather than purely science fiction.

Type: Emerging & Experimental Technology
