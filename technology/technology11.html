Artificial Intelligence

Era: 1950s – Present

Modern artificial intelligence traces back to a 1956 academic conference where the term was first coined, but the field spent decades cycling through periods of excitement and stagnation, sometimes called 'AI winters,' before breakthroughs in machine learning and neural networks in the 2010s finally delivered on long-standing promises. Today's AI systems can generate text, images, and code, diagnose diseases, and drive cars, and the pace of progress has made it one of the most actively debated technologies of the current era, both for its potential and its risks.

Type: Advanced Computing Technology
