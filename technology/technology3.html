Ancient Engineering & Machinery

Era: 3000 BCE – 500 CE

Ancient engineers achieved feats that still puzzle historians today, including Roman concrete that has outlasted much of what modern industry has built in the last century, and the Antikythera mechanism, a Greek device from around 100 BCE now considered the world's first known analog computer, capable of predicting eclipses and tracking astronomical cycles with remarkable precision. It was so far ahead of its time that nothing comparably complex appeared again in the historical record for well over a thousand years.

Type: Early Mechanical Technology
