Printing Press Revolution

Era: 1440 CE

Johannes Gutenberg's printing press, developed around 1440, didn't just speed up book production, it fundamentally restructured how knowledge spread through society, cutting the cost of books dramatically and enabling literacy rates to rise across Europe for the first time in history. Within just 50 years of its invention, an estimated 20 million books had been printed, more than all the scribes in Europe had produced by hand in the previous thousand years combined, fueling the Renaissance, the Reformation, and the eventual Scientific Revolution.
