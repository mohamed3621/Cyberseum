Computers & Digital Technology

Era: 1940s – Present

The first electronic computers in the 1940s, like ENIAC, filled entire rooms, weighed nearly 30 tons, and had a tiny fraction of the processing power of a modern calculator. The invention of the transistor in 1947, followed by the integrated circuit in 1958, triggered a decades-long trend of computers becoming simultaneously smaller, cheaper, and exponentially more powerful, a pattern known as Moore's Law that held remarkably true for over 50 years and reshaped nearly every industry on Earth.

Type: Digital Technology
