Electricity & Early Machines

Era: 1800s

Thomas Edison and Nikola Tesla's rivalry over electrical systems, direct current versus alternating current, became one of history's most consequential technology battles, ultimately won by Tesla and Westinghouse's AC system, which remains the standard for power transmission worldwide today. Electrification transformed daily life at a pace few technologies before or since have matched, extending productive hours past sunset and enabling entirely new categories of machines, from refrigeration to early computing, within just a few decades.

Type: Electrical Technology
