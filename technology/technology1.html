Stone Tools & Early Inventions

Era: 3.4 million – 3000 BCE

The earliest stone tools, found in Kenya, date back roughly 3.3 million years, predating the genus Homo entirely, meaning tool use likely began with earlier hominin ancestors rather than humans as we'd define them today. Early humans gradually developed increasingly sophisticated techniques, from simple sharpened rocks to precisely knapped hand axes, alongside the control of fire roughly a million years ago, a combination that dramatically improved nutrition, safety, and the ability to migrate into colder climates.

Type: Prehistoric Technology
