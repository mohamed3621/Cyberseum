Industrial Revolution

Era: 1760 – 1840

The Industrial Revolution's technological leap centered on harnessing steam power at scale, transforming it from a novelty into the engine, literally, behind factories, railways, and ships. James Watt's improved steam engine design in the 1770s dramatically increased efficiency and became the foundation for mechanized manufacturing that could operate around the clock, independent of human or animal muscle power for the first time in history, permanently changing the scale at which humans could produce goods.

Type: Industrial Technology
