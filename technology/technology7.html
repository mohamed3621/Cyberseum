Telecommunications & Radio

Era: Late 1800s – Early 1900s

Guglielmo Marconi's early radio transmissions in the 1890s and 1900s allowed information to travel instantly across vast distances for the first time in human history, without any physical wire connection at all, a concept that seemed almost magical to contemporaries. Radio played a critical role during both World Wars for coordinating military communication, and by the 1920s and 30s it had become the first true mass broadcast medium, uniting entire nations around shared news and entertainment simultaneously for the first time.

Type: Communication Technology
