Hallstatt, Austria

Location: Salzkammergut Region, Austria

Hallstatt looks almost unreal, a village of pastel houses squeezed between an alpine lake and a steep mountainside, and it's so picturesque that a Chinese company built an exact full-scale replica of the town in Guangdong province in 2012. The area has been continuously inhabited for over 7,000 years, and it gave its name to an entire prehistoric era, the Hallstatt culture, after archaeologists discovered an enormous ancient salt mine nearby that made the region wealthy as far back as 800 BCE.

Type: Mountain Village / Lakeside Landscape
