Reine, Lofoten Islands, Norway

Location: Nordland, Norway

The fishing village of Reine sits in Norway's Lofoten Islands, well above the Arctic Circle, where dramatic granite peaks rise almost directly out of the sea. Thanks to the warming effect of the Gulf Stream, the region has a surprisingly mild climate for its latitude, and in summer it experiences the midnight sun, with daylight lasting nearly 24 hours straight for weeks. In winter, the same location offers some of the most reliable Northern Lights viewing anywhere on Earth.

Type: Arctic Village / Fjord Landscape
