Lake Tekapo, New Zealand

Location: South Island, New Zealand

Lake Tekapo, on New Zealand's South Island, sits within one of the largest officially designated Dark Sky Reserves in the world, where strict lighting regulations keep the night sky remarkably free of light pollution. On clear nights, the Milky Way is visible with striking clarity, and the lake's turquoise color, from glacial rock flour suspended in the water, creates a surreal contrast against surrounding golden tussock grasslands and snow-capped mountains by day.

Type: Lake / Alpine Landscape
