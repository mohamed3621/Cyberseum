Santorini, Greece

Location: Aegean Sea, Greece

Santorini's dramatic cliffs and whitewashed villages sit on the rim of a massive volcanic caldera, the result of one of the largest eruptions in recorded human history around 1600 BCE, an event so violent some historians link it to the legend of Atlantis. The eruption buried a thriving Minoan settlement at Akrotiri under volcanic ash, preserving it almost perfectly, like a Greek Pompeii, complete with multi-story buildings and frescoes. The volcano is still considered active today, and the caldera it created is now one of the most photographed sunset views on Earth.

Type: Island / Coastal Landscape
