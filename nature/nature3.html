Banff National Park, Canada

Location: Alberta, Canada

Banff, Canada's first national park, established in 1885, spans over 6,600 square kilometers of glacier-carved peaks, turquoise lakes, and dense boreal forest. That striking blue-green color in lakes like Moraine and Louise comes from fine rock particles called glacial flour, ground up by moving glaciers and suspended in the meltwater, which reflect light in a way that creates the vivid color. The park is also home to grizzly bears, wolves, and one of the largest populations of elk in North America.

Type: National Park / Mountain Landscape
