Amalfi Coast, Italy

Location: Campania, Italy

The Amalfi Coast's dramatic cliffside towns were historically almost impossible to reach by land, which meant many, like Amalfi and Positano, functioned as isolated, self-sufficient maritime communities for centuries, some even growing into powerful medieval trading republics. The narrow coastal road connecting the towns today, built in the 1800s, is famous for its hairpin turns carved directly into cliff faces plunging into the Tyrrhenian Sea, and it's still considered one of the most dramatic and challenging drives in the world.

Type: Lake / Alpine Landscape
