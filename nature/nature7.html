Chefchaouen, Morocco

Location: Rif Mountains, Morocco

Chefchaouen, tucked into Morocco's Rif Mountains, is famous for its blue-painted buildings, though the exact origin of the tradition is debated. Some link it to Jewish refugees who settled there in the 1930s, who painted buildings blue as a reference to the sky and a traditional symbol of spirituality. Locals now also point out that the blue helps repel mosquitoes and keeps buildings cooler in the heat. Whatever the true origin, the effect is a maze-like old town that feels almost otherworldly to walk through.

Type: Mountain Town / Cultural Landscape
