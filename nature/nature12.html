Bora Bora, French Polynesia

Location: Society Islands, French Polynesia

Bora Bora's lagoon is so clear and shallow around its barrier reef that the water appears to glow in vivid shades of turquoise and teal, a result of white sand reflecting sunlight up through the shallow water. The island is the eroded remnant of an ancient volcano, and its central peaks, Mount Pahia and Mount Otemanu, are all that remain of what was once a much larger volcanic landmass. The surrounding reef has made it one of the most biodiverse snorkeling and diving destinations in the South Pacific.

Type: Tropical Island / Lagoon Landscape
