Plitvice Lakes, Croatia

Location: Central Croatia

Plitvice Lakes, Croatia's oldest national park, is made up of 16 terraced lakes connected by waterfalls, with water that changes color from turquoise to deep blue to green depending on mineral content, algae, and the angle of sunlight. The lakes are separated not by artificial dams but by natural barriers of travertine, a porous limestone rock that's continuously being formed and reshaped by mosses, algae, and bacteria, meaning the landscape itself is technically still changing and growing to this day.

Type: National Park / Waterfall Landscape
