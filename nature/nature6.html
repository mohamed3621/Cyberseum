Antelope Canyon, USA

Location: Arizona, USA

Antelope Canyon in Arizona was carved not slowly over millennia in the usual geological sense, but primarily through sudden, violent flash floods that funnel rushing water and sand through narrow sandstone passageways, smoothing the rock into flowing, wave-like curves. The canyon is on Navajo land and can only be visited with an official Navajo guide. Around midday, sunlight beams down through narrow openings in the rock ceiling in dramatic shafts of light, a phenomenon that draws photographers from around the world to a very specific short window each day.

Type: Slot Canyon / Desert Landscape
