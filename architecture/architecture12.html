Alhambra Palace, Spain

Constructed: 13th–14th century

The Alhambra is a magnificent palace and fortress complex built by the Nasrid dynasty in Granada, Spain. Known for its intricate Islamic art, geometric patterns, serene courtyards, and flowing water features, it represents the height of Moorish architecture in Europe. The Alhambra remains one of Spain’s most treasured cultural landmarks.

Type: Moorish / Islamic Architecture
