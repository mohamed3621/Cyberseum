Bora Bora, French Polynesia

Location: Society Islands, French Polynesia

Bora Bora is a tropical paradise known for its turquoise lagoon, coral reefs, and luxurious overwater bungalows. Surrounded by a ring of small islets and dominated by Mount Otemanu, it is one of the world’s most famous island destinations.

Type: Tropical Island / Lagoon Landscape
