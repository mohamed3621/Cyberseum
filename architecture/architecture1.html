The Great Pyramid of Giza

Constructed: c. 2580–2560 BCE

The Great Pyramid of Giza is the largest and oldest of the three pyramids on the Giza Plateau in Egypt. Built as a monumental tomb for the Pharaoh Khufu, it originally stood at 146.6 meters and remained the tallest human‑made structure in the world for over 3,800 years. Constructed from millions of limestone blocks, the pyramid is a masterpiece of ancient engineering and the only surviving Wonder of the Ancient World.

Type: Ancient Egyptian Architecture
