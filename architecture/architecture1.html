The Great Pyramid of Giza

Constructed: c. 2580–2560 BCE

Rising 146 meters above the desert, the Great Pyramid was the tallest structure on Earth for nearly 4,000 years, and it's still not fully understood how it was built. Ancient workers moved over 2 million limestone blocks, some weighing as much as nine tons, without wheels, pulleys, or iron tools. Inside, hidden chambers and narrow shafts point toward distant stars, part of a burial complex designed to send Pharaoh Khufu into the afterlife. Modern scans have even revealed a mysterious void deep within the structure that archaeologists still can't fully explain.

Type: Ancient Egyptian Architecture
