Hagia Sophia, Türkiye

Constructed: 532–537 CE

Hagia Sophia is a monumental architectural masterpiece located in Istanbul. Originally built as a Byzantine cathedral, it later became an Ottoman mosque and now serves as a mosque and cultural landmark. Its massive dome, golden mosaics, and fusion of architectural styles make it one of the most influential buildings in world history.

Type: Byzantine & Ottoman Architecture
