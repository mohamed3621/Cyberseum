Angkor Wat, Cambodia

Constructed: Early 12th century

Angkor Wat is the largest religious monument in the world and the crown jewel of the Khmer Empire. Originally built as a Hindu temple dedicated to Vishnu, it later became a Buddhist site. Its grand towers, intricate bas‑reliefs, and symmetrical layout reflect extraordinary architectural mastery. Surrounded by lush forests and a vast moat, Angkor Wat remains a symbol of Cambodia’s cultural heritage.

Type: Khmer Temple Architecture
