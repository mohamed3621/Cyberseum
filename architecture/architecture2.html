The Parthenon, Greece

Constructed: 447–432 BCE

The Parthenon is an ancient Greek temple located on the Acropolis of Athens, dedicated to the goddess Athena. Built during the height of the Athenian Empire, it is considered one of the greatest achievements of Classical Greek architecture. Its harmonious proportions, Doric columns, and intricate sculptural decorations reflect the artistic and cultural brilliance of ancient Greece. Today, the Parthenon stands as a symbol of democracy, philosophy, and Western civilization.

Type: Classical Greek Architecture
