The Great Wall of China

Constructed: 7th century BCE – 17th century CE

The Great Wall isn't actually one continuous wall. It's a sprawling network of walls, watchtowers, and fortifications built by different dynasties over roughly 2,000 years, stretching a combined 21,000 kilometers if every branch is measured. Soldiers stationed along it used smoke signals by day and fire by night to relay warnings of invasion across hundreds of kilometers in hours. Despite the popular myth, it isn't actually visible from space with the naked eye, but standing on one of its ancient ridgelines still gives a sense of the scale of the effort behind it.

Type: Ancient Defensive Architecture
