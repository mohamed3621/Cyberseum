The Great Wall of China

Constructed: 7th century BCE – 17th century CE

The Great Wall of China is a vast defensive structure built across northern China to protect against invasions. Spanning thousands of kilometers, it includes watchtowers, fortresses, and winding stone pathways. Constructed by multiple dynasties, most famously the Ming, it stands as one of the most impressive engineering feats in human history.

Type: Ancient Defensive Architecture
