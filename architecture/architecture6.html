Petra, Jordan

Constructed: c. 5th century BCE

Petra, known as the “Rose‑Red City,” is an ancient Nabataean capital carved directly into sandstone cliffs. Its most famous structure, Al‑Khazneh (The Treasury), features an ornate façade blending Hellenistic and Middle Eastern styles. Petra served as a major trading hub and remains one of the most breathtaking archaeological sites in the world.

Type: Rock‑Cut Architecture / Nabataean Civilization
