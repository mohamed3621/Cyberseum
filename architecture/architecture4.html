The Colosseum, Rome

Constructed: 70–80 CE

Nearly 2,000 years ago, the Colosseum could fill with 50,000 spectators in under ten minutes using a system of 80 numbered entrances, a feat modern stadiums still study. Beneath the arena floor, a maze of tunnels and elevators hauled gladiators and wild animals up through trapdoors mid-fight. The Romans even flooded the entire arena to stage mock naval battles. Despite earthquakes, fires, and stone robbers who stripped its marble for centuries, enough of it survives to still hint at the scale of ancient Rome's engineering ambition.

Type: Ancient Roman Architecture
