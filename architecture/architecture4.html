The Colosseum, Rome

Constructed: 70–80 CE

The Colosseum is an enormous amphitheater built in the heart of ancient Rome. Designed for gladiatorial contests, public spectacles, and dramatic performances, it could hold up to 50,000 spectators. Its advanced engineering, including vaulted corridors and tiered seating, showcases the architectural brilliance of the Roman Empire. Today, it stands as one of the most iconic monuments of ancient history.

Type: Ancient Roman Architecture
