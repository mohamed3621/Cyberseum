Neuschwanstein Castle, Germany

Constructed: 1869–1886

Neuschwanstein Castle is a fairy‑tale palace built by King Ludwig II of Bavaria. Perched on a rugged hilltop, it features romantic towers, sweeping views, and lavish interiors inspired by medieval legends. Its dreamlike appearance later inspired Disney’s iconic castle design.

Type: Romanesque Revival Architecture
