Taj Mahal, India

Constructed: 1632–1653

The Taj Mahal is a white marble mausoleum built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. Renowned for its perfect symmetry, intricate carvings, and reflective pools, it is considered one of the most beautiful buildings ever created. The Taj Mahal is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a symbol of eternal love.

Type: Mughal Architecture
