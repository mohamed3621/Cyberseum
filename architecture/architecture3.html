Machu Picchu, Peru

Constructed: c. 1450 CE

Hidden in the Andes at 2,430 meters, Machu Picchu stayed unknown to the outside world for centuries, its stone walls swallowed by jungle until a local guide led an American historian there in 1911. The Inca built it without mortar or iron tools, carving granite blocks so precisely they still resist earthquakes today. No one is entirely sure why it was built. Theories range from royal retreat to sacred astronomical observatory, and the mystery is part of why over a million visitors climb up to see it every year.

Type: Incan Architecture / Archaeological Site
