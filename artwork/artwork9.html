Lady With An Ermine

Created: c. 1489–1490

Leonardo da Vinci painted Lady with an Ermine as a portrait of Cecilia Gallerani, the teenage mistress of the Duke of Milan, making it one of the first portraits in Western art to show a subject twisting dynamically rather than posed stiffly forward. X-ray analysis has revealed the ermine itself was added in later stages of painting, and may symbolize both purity and the duke himself, whose personal emblem was an ermine. It's one of only four confirmed female portraits by da Vinci still in existence.

Type: Renaissance Portrait Painting
