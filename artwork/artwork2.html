The Starry Night

Created: 1889

Portrait of a Night Sky, known as The Starry Night – Created by Vincent van Gogh

*The Starry Night* is one of Vincent van Gogh’s most iconic masterpieces, painted during his stay at the Saint‑Paul‑de‑Mausole asylum in Saint‑Rémy‑de‑Provence, France. The swirling night sky, glowing moon, and vibrant stars reflect van Gogh’s expressive style and emotional depth. The painting blends imagination with observation, capturing a dramatic and dreamlike vision of the night.

A swirling, dream‑like vision of the night, The Starry Night is one of van Gogh’s most iconic works. Painted in 1889 while he was staying at the Saint‑Rémy asylum, it captures the emotional intensity of the landscape rather than a literal view. Its bold spirals and glowing stars have made it a symbol of van Gogh’s unique way of seeing the world.

Type: Post‑Impressionist Oil Painting
