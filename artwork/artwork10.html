The Scream

Created: 1893

Expression of Raw Emotion, known as The Scream – Created by Edvard Munch

*The Scream* is one of the most iconic works of Expressionist art, created by Norwegian painter Edvard Munch. The painting portrays a figure clutching its face in anguish beneath a swirling, fiery sky. Munch described the scene as capturing a moment of overwhelming existential anxiety. Its bold colors, dramatic lines, and emotional intensity have made it a universal symbol of human fear and inner turmoil.

A haunting vision of anxiety and inner turmoil, The Scream (1893) shows a figure crying out beneath a swirling, blood‑red sky. Munch based it on a moment when he felt “a great scream pass through nature,” and the painting’s distorted lines and intense colors capture that overwhelming feeling. It has become one of the most recognizable images of human emotion in modern art.

Type: Expressionist Painting
