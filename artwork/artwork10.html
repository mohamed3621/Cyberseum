The Scream

Created: 1893

Edvard Munch said the inspiration for The Scream came from a real moment walking at sunset when he felt a 'great scream through nature,' describing it almost as a panic attack rendered in paint. He made several versions of the image, including one in pastel that later became the most expensive artwork ever sold at auction at the time, in 2012. The painting has been stolen twice from Norwegian museums, once in 1994 and again in 2004, both times eventually recovered, cementing its reputation as one of the most famous and most targeted paintings in the world.

Type: Expressionist Painting
