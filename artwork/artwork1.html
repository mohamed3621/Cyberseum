Mona Lisa

Created: c. 1503–1519

Leonardo da Vinci worked on the Mona Lisa for over a decade, and some historians believe he never actually considered it finished, carrying it with him until his death. The painting's famous smile seems to shift depending on where you look, an optical effect created by da Vinci's soft-edged sfumato technique blending tones without visible lines. It was stolen from the Louvre in 1911 by a former employee who hid it in his apartment for two years, and the theft made the painting into the global icon it is today.

Type: Renaissance Portrait Painting
