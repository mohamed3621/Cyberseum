Mona Lisa

Created: c. 1503–1519

Portrait of Lisa Gherardini, known as the Mona Lisa in the Salle des États - Created by Leonardo da Vinci

The Mona Lisa is a world‑famous portrait painted by Leonardo da Vinci during the Italian Renaissance. Known for its subtle expression, detailed realism, and innovative use of sfumato, the painting depicts Lisa Gherardini, a Florentine woman. Today, it is displayed in the Louvre Museum in Paris and is considered one of the most recognized and influential artworks in history.

The world’s most famous painting, the Mona Lisa. Mona Lisa’s famously enigmatic smile has fascinated viewers for centuries. Among her first admirers was King François I, who invited Leonardo da Vinci to France and bought the painting from him in 1518.

Type: Renaissance Portrait Painting
