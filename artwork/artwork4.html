The Waterlily Pond with the Japanese Bridge

Created: 1899

Monet built the Japanese footbridge and water garden at his home in Giverny himself, then spent the last 30 years of his life painting it obsessively, producing over 250 studies of the same pond. As his eyesight deteriorated from cataracts later in life, his brushwork grew looser and his colors more intense, some scholars believe his vision loss directly shaped the increasingly abstract style seen in his final water lily paintings. The garden still exists today in France, maintained to look almost exactly as it did when he painted it.

Type: Impressionist Landscape Painting
