Girl With A Pearl Earring

Created: c. 1665

Portrait of a Young Woman, known as Girl with a Pearl Earring – Created by Johannes Vermeer

*Girl with a Pearl Earring* is one of the most celebrated works by Dutch painter Johannes Vermeer. Often called the “Mona Lisa of the North,” the painting features a young woman wearing an exotic turban and a striking pearl earring. Its soft lighting, delicate expression, and masterful use of color and shadow make it a defining example of Dutch Golden Age portraiture. The artwork is housed in the Mauritshuis museum in The Hague, Netherlands.

A quiet, luminous study of light and mystery, Girl with a Pearl Earring is Vermeer’s most celebrated work. Painted around 1665, it isn’t a traditional portrait but a tronie — a study of a character rather than a specific person. Her turned pose, soft gaze, and the glowing pearl create an intimate moment that feels surprisingly modern.

Type: Dutch Golden Age Portrait
