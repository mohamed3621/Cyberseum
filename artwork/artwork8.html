A Pathway in Monet's Garden

Created: 1902

This lesser-known Monet painting captures the same garden path at Giverny that appears in dozens of his other works, but from a different angle and season, showing how obsessively he returned to the same few square meters of land for decades. Monet designed the garden itself as a living artwork, planting and arranging flowers specifically with color combinations for painting in mind rather than traditional garden design. Walking through it today, visitors can recognize the exact path and archway shown in this piece.

Type: Impressionist Landscape Painting
