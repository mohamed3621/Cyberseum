Bouquet of Sunflowers

Created: 1881

Van Gogh painted several versions of sunflowers to decorate the room of his friend and fellow artist Paul Gauguin before Gauguin's visit to Arles, hoping the bright yellow would create a warm welcome. He used a newly available and highly unstable yellow pigment, chrome yellow, which has actually darkened and browned over time, meaning the painting we see today looks noticeably different from how it appeared when freshly painted. Van Gogh considered the sunflower series some of his best work, a rare moment of genuine confidence in an often difficult career.

Type: Impressionist Still‑Life Painting
