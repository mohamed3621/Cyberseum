Starry Night Over The Rhone

Created: 1888

View of a Starlit Riverside, known as Starry Night Over the Rhône – Created by Vincent van Gogh

*Starry Night Over the Rhône* is an atmospheric nighttime scene painted by Vincent van Gogh in Arles, France. The artwork captures the reflections of gas lamps shimmering across the Rhône River beneath a sky filled with bright stars. Van Gogh’s expressive brushwork and rich blues and yellows create a peaceful yet emotionally charged depiction of the riverside at night. It is one of his most celebrated explorations of light and color.

A calm, shimmering night scene painted in 1888, Starry Night Over the Rhône shows van Gogh’s love for color and reflection. The deep blue sky, glowing stars, and golden lights dancing on the water create a peaceful mood, while the couple walking along the river adds a quiet human touch. It’s one of his most poetic works from his time in Arles.

Type: Post‑Impressionist Landscape Painting
