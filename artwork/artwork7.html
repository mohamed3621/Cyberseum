The Last Supper

Created: 1495–1498

Leonardo da Vinci painted The Last Supper directly onto a dry wall using an experimental technique instead of true fresco, which requires painting on wet plaster. It was a decision that let him work slowly and revise details, but it also meant the painting began flaking and deteriorating within just a few decades of completion. Napoleon's troops later used the room as a stable, and the painting survived a WWII bombing that destroyed the rest of the building around it. Despite centuries of damage and restoration, it remains one of the most studied compositions in art history.

Type: Renaissance Mural Painting
