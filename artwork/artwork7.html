The Last Supper

Created: 1495–1498

Depiction of a Sacred Gathering, known as The Last Supper – Created by Leonardo da Vinci

*The Last Supper* is a monumental mural painting by Leonardo da Vinci, created for the Convent of Santa Maria delle Grazie in Milan, Italy. The artwork depicts the dramatic moment when Jesus announces that one of his disciples will betray him. Leonardo’s masterful use of perspective, composition, and emotional expression makes it one of the most influential and studied works in Western art history.

A dramatic and deeply symbolic mural painted around 1495–1498, The Last Supper captures the moment Jesus tells his disciples that one of them will betray him. Leonardo arranges the figures in expressive groups, using gesture, light, and perspective to heighten the emotional tension. The composition’s balance and storytelling have made it one of the most studied works in Western art.

Type: Renaissance Mural Painting
