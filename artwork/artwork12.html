Stańczyk

Created: 1862

Portrait of a Melancholic Jester, known as Stańczyk – Created by Jan Matejko

*Stańczyk* is a powerful historical painting by Polish artist Jan Matejko. It portrays the famous royal jester Stańczyk sitting alone in deep contemplation during a grand ball. While others celebrate in the background, Stańczyk reflects on the tragic news of Poland’s political losses. The painting symbolizes wisdom, patriotism, and the burden of understanding national tragedy, making it one of Matejko’s most emotionally resonant works.

A striking and introspective work from 1862, Stańczyk shows the famous Polish court jester sitting alone during a royal celebration. While others celebrate in the background, he broods over a tragic political event, symbolizing the weight of national sorrow. Matejko uses the jester’s expression and the dim lighting to turn him into a symbol of wisdom, patriotism, and quiet despair.

Type: Historical Realist Painting
