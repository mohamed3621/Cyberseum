The Renaissance

Period: 1300 – 1600 CE

The Renaissance began in 14th-century Florence, fueled by wealthy patrons like the Medici family and a renewed fascination with classical Greek and Roman texts rediscovered through contact with the Islamic world. It produced an extraordinary concentration of genius in a short span, Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, Raphael, and Galileo all lived within roughly the same era. The invention of the printing press around 1440 dramatically accelerated the spread of these new ideas across Europe, arguably making the Renaissance possible on the scale it reached.

Type: Cultural & Intellectual Movement
