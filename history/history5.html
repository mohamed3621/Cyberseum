Ancient Greece

Period: 800 – 146 BCE

Ancient Greece gave the world democracy, theater, the Olympic Games, and the foundations of Western philosophy, science, and mathematics, all from a relatively small collection of independent city-states that frequently warred with each other. Athenian democracy, while revolutionary, only extended voting rights to free adult male citizens, a small fraction of the population. Greek thinkers like Pythagoras, Socrates, and Aristotle asked questions about the nature of reality, ethics, and the universe that philosophers and scientists are, in many ways, still trying to answer today.

Type: Classical Civilization
