The Middle Ages

Period: 476 – 1453 CE

The Middle Ages, spanning roughly 1,000 years after Rome's fall, is often unfairly dismissed as a 'dark age,' but it was a period of major innovation, including the university system, mechanical clocks, eyeglasses, and the printing press near its end. The Black Death, arriving in 1347, killed an estimated 30 to 60 percent of Europe's population within just a few years, a demographic catastrophe so severe it fundamentally reshaped labor, wages, and social structures across the continent for generations afterward.

Type: Medieval Historical Era
