The Modern Era

Period: 1900s – Present

The Modern Era, roughly the last century and a half, has seen more technological change than all of prior human history combined, from powered flight and electricity to computers, the internet, and space travel, all within a few human lifetimes. Two world wars reshaped global politics and borders, while decolonization movements across Africa and Asia redrew the map yet again in the mid-20th century. It's the era we're still living through, which makes it uniquely difficult to say with confidence what will end up mattering most to history.

Type: Contemporary Historical Period
