Ancient Egypt

Period: 3100 – 30 BCE

Ancient Egyptian civilization lasted an astonishing 3,000 years, longer than the time separating us from Julius Caesar, and it was built almost entirely around the predictable annual flooding of the Nile, which the Egyptians tracked with remarkable astronomical precision. Their belief in the afterlife drove some of the most ambitious engineering projects in human history, including the pyramids, and their mummification process was sophisticated enough that scientists today can still study the DNA and diseases of people who died thousands of years ago.

Type: Ancient Civilization
