The Age of Exploration

Period: 1400s – 1700s

The Age of Exploration was driven largely by Europe's search for a direct sea route to Asian spice markets, bypassing expensive overland trade routes controlled by middlemen. It led to genuinely historic achievements in navigation and shipbuilding, but also catastrophic consequences, including the transatlantic slave trade and the deaths of an estimated 90 percent of Indigenous populations in the Americas from European diseases within just a century of contact. It's remembered as an era of discovery, but its full legacy is far more complicated than that framing suggests.

Type: Global Exploration Era
