The Ice Age

Period: 2.4 million – 11,700 years ago

The Ice Age wasn't one single event but a series of repeating glacial and warm periods stretching back 2.4 million years, with ice sheets at times covering nearly a third of Earth's land surface. Sea levels dropped so low during peak glaciation that land bridges connected continents, letting early humans and animals migrate across areas now covered by ocean, including the route into the Americas across the Bering land bridge. Woolly mammoths, saber-toothed cats, and giant ground sloths all thrived during this era, and some, like mammoths, coexisted with early humans who hunted them.

Type: Prehistoric Climate Era
