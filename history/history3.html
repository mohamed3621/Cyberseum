Ancient Mesopotamia

Period: 3500 – 539 BCE

Ancient Mesopotamia, located between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers, is often called the “Cradle of Civilization.” It saw the rise of the first cities, writing systems like cuneiform, early laws, and major empires such as Sumer, Babylon, and Assyria. Its innovations shaped the foundations of modern society.

Type: Early Civilization
