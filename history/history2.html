The Stone Age

Period: 3.4 million – 3300 BCE

The Stone Age spans an enormous stretch of human history, from the first crude stone tools nearly 3.3 million years ago to the rise of metalworking, and it's typically divided into the Paleolithic, Mesolithic, and Neolithic periods. It was during this era that humans first controlled fire, developed spoken language, created cave art, and, near its end, transitioned from nomadic hunting and gathering to permanent farming settlements, arguably the single most transformative shift in human history. Sites like Göbekli Tepe in Turkey suggest organized religious structures existed even before farming did.

Type: Prehistoric Human Era
