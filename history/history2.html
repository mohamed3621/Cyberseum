The Stone Age

Period: 3.4 million – 3300 BCE

The Stone Age marks the earliest known period of human history, defined by the creation of stone tools. During this era, humans developed hunting, gathering, early art, and the first permanent settlements. It is divided into the Paleolithic, Mesolithic, and Neolithic periods, each showing major steps in human progress.

Type: Prehistoric Human Era
